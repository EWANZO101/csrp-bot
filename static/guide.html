For players

Player guides

Money, characters, shopping, jobs, the hospital and your bills.

👋

Getting started

Your account, starting money and where things are

  1. You already have an account — every member gets one with a starting balance in the bank.
  2. Type /balance to see your 💵 wallet and 🏦 bank.
  3. Type /bank for a menu with buttons for everything money-related.
  4. Browse the 🎮 CSRP Player Hub channels — each one has buttons, so you rarely need to type commands.
  5. Stuck? Open a ticket in 🎫・support.

TipMoney in your wallet can be spent anywhere; money in the bank is safer.

💵

Bank, wallet & paying people

Deposit, withdraw, pay someone, see history

  1. Deposit wallet → bank: /deposit amount:500 (or all).
  2. Withdraw bank → wallet: /withdraw amount:500.
  3. Pay someone: /pay user:@name amount:250 — you can add a note.
  4. See what happened: /history shows your recent transactions.
  5. Richest players: /leaderboard.

TipAmounts like 5k, 2.5m and all work everywhere.

🎭

Characters

Play several characters, each with their own money & items

  1. Go to 🎭・characters and press ➕ Create (or /character create name:Tony).
  2. Choose whether to switch to them now. New characters start with the starting balance and an empty inventory.
  3. Press 🔁 Switch any time to change who you're playing. The other character's money and items wait safely.
  4. 📋 My characters shows each one's wallet, bank and items.
  5. ✏️ Rename or 🗑️ Delete extra characters (delete asks first; the main character can't be deleted).

TipMoney imported from the old bot always goes to your main character.

🛍️

Shopping

Find and buy things from businesses

  1. See everything for sale: /shop all.
  2. Look inside one shop: /shop browse store: and start typing the business name — pick from the list.
  3. Buy from the menu that appears, or type /shop buy store:… item:… quantity:1.
  4. The money comes out of your bank and the item goes into your inventory.

TipIf a shop says out of stock, ask the owner to restock.

🎒

Inventory, giving & using items

See your items, give them away, use them

  1. See your items: /inventory (or /inventory user:@name for someone else).
  2. Give an item: /give user:@name item: — start typing and pick the item, then the quantity.
  3. Use an item: /use item: — some items give you a role for a while, some get used up.
🏢

Owning a business

Get a business, manage money, staff and stock

  1. Ask for a business in 🏢・business-requests (press 📝) — staff approve it and it's yours.
  2. Put money in: /business deposit · take it out: /business withdraw · pay someone: /business send.
  3. Add things to sell: /business item add (price, stock, description). Edit with /business item edit.
  4. Give staff access: /business role add (head or member) and pay them with /payroll add.
  5. See it all: /business info · hand it over: /business transfer_ownership.

TipShop sales go straight into your business account.

💼

Getting paid (payroll)

How wages work

  1. Your boss adds you with /payroll add and sets your pay.
  2. Pay arrives in your bank automatically on payday (or when they run /payroll run).
  3. You get a DM each payday. Check with /history.

TipNot paid? The business may be out of money — tell your boss.

🧾

Citations & fines

See and pay fines

  1. See your fines: /citation mine.
  2. Pay one: /citation pay id:12 (from your bank), or press 💳 Pay now in the DM you got.
  3. Pay before the due date — overdue fines get a late fee.

TipMedical bills from the hospital show here too.

🏥

Hospital (patient)

What happens when you're admitted and how to pay

  1. When medics admit you, you get a DM and the clock starts — you're billed per second while admitted.
  2. Every 30 seconds the cost so far is taken from your bank. If you run out, the rest becomes debt.
  3. Check your bill any time: 💳 My bill in 🏥・hospital (or /hospital bill).
  4. When you're discharged, anything unpaid becomes a medical bill — pay it with the 💳 button in the DM or /citation pay.

TipKeep some money in the bank — it's used first.

💡

Utilities (power, water, phone…)

Set up payment, sign up, pay bills

  1. In 💡・utilities press 💳 Payment menu first — pick bank autopay, wallet autopay or manual, plus a backup. Press Save.
  2. Press ➕ Sign up, choose a company, then a plan, then Sign up.
  3. Bills arrive every cycle. With autopay they're paid automatically (and cheaper!).
  4. 📄 My bills shows what you owe — pay one or Pay all.
  5. Late? A late fee is added, and after a while the service is shut off. Pay up, then press 🔌 Reconnect in 🔌 My services.

TipAutopay retries automatically as soon as you have money.

🏠

Requesting a property or business

Ask staff for a house or a business

  1. Go to 🏠・property-requests (or 🏢・business-requests).
  2. Press 📝 and fill in what you want.
  3. Staff approve or deny it — you get a DM either way. There may be a price, taken from your bank.
  4. Approved properties appear in your inventory; approved businesses are yours to run.

Support, applications & documents

Get help, apply for jobs, read rules

  1. Need help? Open a ticket in 🎫・support.
  2. Apply for a job or role: 📝・applications or /apply.
  3. Rules, SOPs, penal codes: 📚・documents or /docs.

Events & who's online

Server events and bot activity

  2. 📈・bot-activity shows who's using the bot right now and the most active this week.
  3. Type /active to see it privately.
For staff

Staff guides

Setup and admin. Every staff button and command checks your permissions, so reading these is safe for anyone.

🔑

Giving someone staff access

Staff ranks and who can see staff channels

  1. Open the web panel https://bot.snailyapp.com and log in with Discord.
  2. Go to Staff → pick a rank (Admin, Moderator, Support…) → add their role or user.
  3. Each rank has permissions: Citizens & money, Businesses, Citations, Server setup…
  4. In Discord run /staff_panel and /player_accounts so the staff channels let them in.

TipBot owners always have every permission.

🛠️

Using the staff panel

Everything in #🛠️・staff-panel

  1. Go to 🛠️・staff-panel and press a button — a form pops up.
  2. Player boxes: start typing a name and pick from the list.
  3. Business / item boxes: type part of the name — the closest match is used (the reply tells you which).
  4. Replies are only visible to you; money and item changes are posted to 📒・bot-logs.

TipThe 🏥 Hospital admin card lives in the same channel.

💰

Money: print, remove, freeze, undo

Fix balances and mistakes

  1. Give money: 💰 Print money in the staff panel, or /admin print amount:5000 user:@name.
  2. Take money: 🔥 Remove money, or /admin remove.
  3. Freeze an account (blocks spending): 🧊 in the panel or /admin freeze.
  4. Undo a transaction: find its # with /admin audit (or 🧾 Transactions), then /admin undo id:123.

TipFor a business, leave the player empty and type the business name.

📦

Moving items between players

Fix inventories or transfer everything

  1. 📦 Move items in the staff panel (or /admin move_items).
  2. Pick from and to players. Type an item, or leave it empty to move everything.
  3. Optional quantity — empty means all of it.
  4. Also on the web panel: Citizens → click a player → Move items.
🏢

Businesses, departments & shops

Create them and set up what they sell

  1. Create a department: /department create · a business: /business create (or approve a business request).
  2. Add shop items: 🛒 Add / edit shop item in the staff panel, or /business item add.
  3. Give an item from stock to a player for free: 🎁 Give item from stock.
  4. Change an owner: 👑 Set business owner. See details: 🏢 Business info.
💼

Payroll

Paying staff of businesses & departments

  1. Add someone: /payroll add org:… user:@name amount:500.
  2. Pay now: /payroll run. Automatic payroll runs every N hours — change with /admin config key:payroll_hours value:168 (0 = manual).
  3. Want a fixed day/time instead? Use /schedule payroll when:fri 18:00 repeat:Weekly and set payroll_hours to 0.
🏥

Running the hospital

Billing settings, admitting, bills

  1. In 🛠️・staff-panel → 🏥 Hospital admin press ⚙️ Billing settings: set the hospital department (who gets paid), cost per second, admission fee, cap and days to pay.
  2. Medics use 🏥 Admit patient / 🩺 Discharge in 🏥・hospital (or /hospital admit|discharge).
  3. Medics = members of the hospital department, or staff with Citations / Citizens & money.
  4. See unpaid bills (🧾), cancel one (💸 Waive a bill) or view 📊 Stats in the admin card.

TipIf no department is set, medical payments just disappear.

💡

Setting up utilities

Companies, plans, rules, opening it up

  1. Open 💡・utilities-admin and press 🌱 Starter pack — 7 companies with plans are created.
  2. Press 🏢 Company for each one and set the business/department that gets paid.
  3. Adjust 📦 Plans (price, cycle, usage swing, setup fee) and ⚙️ Billing rules (late fee, days to pay, shut-off, reconnect fee, autopay discount).
  4. Test it yourself in 💡・utilities (staff can use it while locked).
  5. When ready press 🔓 Open to players.

Tip📊 Overview shows customers, revenue and who owes.

🗓️

Scheduling things

Buttons in #🗓️・schedule, or slash commands

  1. Open 🗓️・schedule. Staff see buttons: 📅 Add event, 📣 Announcement, 💸 Payment, 💼 Payroll, 🗂️ Manage and 🌍 Timezone.
  2. Press 🌍 Timezone once and type yours, e.g. America/New_York.
  3. Press a button, fill in the form and choose how often it repeats. When can be in 2h, 20:00, tomorrow 8pm or fri 19:30.
  4. Events show on the schedule card with a countdown, a reminder before they start and a starting now post.
  5. 🗂️ Manage lets you pause, resume, run now or delete anything that's scheduled.
  6. Prefer commands? /schedule announce, /schedule money, /schedule event, /schedule payroll and /schedule list do the same.

TipTimes: in 2h, 20:00, tomorrow 8pm, fri 19:30, 2026-11-20 18:00.

📝

Business & property requests

Approve, deny and open the channels

  1. Requests appear in 🏢・business-requests-admin / 🏠・property-requests-admin with ✅ Approve / ❌ Deny.
  2. When approving you can set a price (taken from the player's bank) and a note.
  3. Approved business → created and owned by the player. Approved property → added to their inventory.
  4. The player channels start private — open them from the admin card when you're ready.
📥

Importing from the old bot

Bring balances, items and shops across

  1. In #banks-inventory run the old bot's /balance user:@player — it's imported within seconds.
  2. Inventories: run its inventory command there and click through every page (Next, Next…).
  3. Shops: run business_inventory for each business.
  4. Nothing doubles — the first card sets the value, later cards only apply changes.
  5. Images posted there are saved; ask Claude to read new data and they're imported the same safe way.

TipEvery import is posted in 📒・bot-logs.

Player accounts directory

Everyone's money & items in one place

  1. Open 📊・player-accounts — one card, richest first, 20 per page.
  2. Use ◀ Prev / Next ▶ to page through.
  3. Type a name in 🔎 to see that player's full account (all items, businesses) — only you see it.
  4. Updates every minute. /player_accounts refreshes it now.

Characters (staff)

Limits and viewing players' characters

  1. Change the limit: /admin config key:max_characters value:3.
  2. See anyone's characters: /character list user:@name.
  3. Each character has its own wallet, bank and inventory; businesses, payroll and citations stay with the Discord user.

Issuing citations

Fines for department members

  1. Department members: /citation issue org:LSPD user:@name amount:500 reason:….
  2. Department heads can /citation edit and /citation void.
  3. Players pay with /citation pay; overdue ones get the late fee (/admin config key:late_fee).

Tickets, applications & documents

Posting the panels

  1. Tickets: /ticket panel in the channel you want.
  2. Applications: /apply_panel — build the forms on the web panel.
  3. Documents: /docs_panel — add documents on the web panel.
  4. Settings for all of these: web panel → Settings.
